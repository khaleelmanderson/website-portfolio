Freelance Branding & Content Project

I am currently collaborating with a group of friends who are building a SaaS company and needed professional branding materials to showcase their latest product — a ticketing platform designed for small businesses seeking an efficient and affordable alternative to major corporations with high priced options. I designed a branded brochure using Canva and Adobe InDesign, developing the layout, typography, copywriting, and visual identity to align with the company's goals and audience.

  • Applied visual storytelling and content strategy principles
  • Used Canva, Adobe InDesign, and After Effects
  • Ongoing collaboration as the product continues to develop
